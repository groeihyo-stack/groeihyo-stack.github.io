11 places nearby, and the people behind them

Itaewon grew up around the foreign communities that settled near the US military base standing beside it since the 1940s. A German chef, two friends from South Africa, a pitmaster raised in Alabama, and three Texans who once ran a taco truck all arrived in this neighborhood and stayed — some for over a decade, one for more than fifty years. Itaewon Foreign Bookstore has been run by the same family since 1973, when its founder was still selling English-language books from a handcart. Seoul's vegan food scene has roots here too: Plant Cafe Seoul grew out of a blog its founder started while learning to cook without meat. The eleven places in this guide are the ones we could confirm through interviews and public records, told through the people who run them.

The Baker's Table 더 베이커스 테이블

Best for Breakfast · German breadWalk from UMMU 12 min · 780 m

Address
244-1 Noksapyeong-daero (녹사평대로 244-1)
The story
Micha Richter, a German chef, first came to Seoul on assignment at a five-star hotel and has now lived in the city for 25 years. Every Christmas season he bakes stollen using the recipe his father and grandfather made, marinating the dried fruit in rum and watching the dough's temperature and consistency by hand. As he told the Korea JoongAng Daily, rushing the process or skimping on the butter, candied fruit, raisins, rum, flour, and spices misses what makes a stollen right.
Order this
German breads — Hard Roll, Bretzel, Vollkorn (whole-grain loaf); stollen in the Christmas season.
Say this in Korean
슈톨렌 있어요?Syutollen isseoyo? · Do you have stollen?
Good to know
The breakfast spot near the house. Stollen is seasonal (December).

The story
Mipa Lee grew up moving between the United States, the United Kingdom, Ghana, and Côte d'Ivoire before settling in Seoul. In 2009 she started a blog, Alien's Day Out, to document going vegan — first for environmental reasons, and then, as she learned more about factory farming, for animal welfare as well. Mr. Ahn's Makgeolli 한국술집 안씨막걸리

Best for Dinner · Korean food & rice wineWalk from UMMU 11 min · 700 m

Address
1F, 3 Hoenamu-ro (회나무로 3 아름누리빌딩 1층)
The story
Ahn Sang-hyun had no restaurant experience — he had worked in consulting, at the e-commerce company TMON, and in politics — before opening a Korean rice-wine restaurant. He has said the idea came after roughly two years of drinking almost every night, when he discovered high-end Korean liquor and decided to brew and sell his own craft makgeolli. The first year and a half were hard enough that he lived in a single room above the shop and then in a cheap basement room, and it took him 18 months to reach 30 million won in monthly sales.
Order this
The house-brewed makgeolli, with tofu platter (두부한상), squid sundae (한치순대), or grilled rice cake (가래떡구이) to finish.

Good to know
Dinner only. Enter through the wooden door next to Starbucks. Listed in the Michelin Guide Seoul 2026; book ahead on Catchtable or Naver — when it's full, tables are limited to two hours.

Linus Kim grew up in Birmingham, Alabama, and has said that as a picky-eating kid, the pulled pork sandwich he tasted became his first favorite food. Roddy Bancroft and Chris Truter came to Seoul to play rugby and, in 2006, began making biltong and boerewors — South African cured meat and sausage — for themselves. They opened Braai Republic in Itaewon in 2011, and Bancroft has said he first came to Korea as an English teacher and only later realized he had ended up staying almost 20 years. As he told Forbes Africa, staying was about reciprocity: "they've looked after us here, and so we look after them."

Best for Dinner · Korean barbecueWalk from UMMU 12 min · 690 m

Address
1F, 7 Bogwang-ro 60-gil (보광로60길 7 1층)
The story
Yoo Ji-young opened Tongue & Groove Joint in a back alley in Itaewon in 2016, designing the boutique-style restaurant to feel avant-garde rather than like a typical barbecue place. She told the Korea Herald that the interior is built on "the properties of matters that are closest to the essence" — an idea she drew from traditional Korean woodworking and rafters — because she believes people can be fully happy with materials alone, without an exaggerated ambience. She said she envisioned a place where both customers and staff "could act like themselves without caring what others thought," adding that she doesn't believe a Korean restaurant has to be all-out Korean — "why can't a Korean restaurant play hip-hop music?"
Order this
Dry-aged hanwoo sirloin or aged pork belly, then the kimchi beef fried rice to finish.
Say this in Korean
삼겹살이랑 양갈비 주세요Samgyeopsal-irang yanggalbi juseyo · Pork belly and lamb chops, please
Good to know
Staff grill the meat at your table. A 2-minute walk from Itaewon Station Exit 3; reservations on Catchtable.

Choi Ki-woong opened Itaewon Foreign Bookstore in 1973, after years of collecting English-language books, catalogs, and magazines discarded near US military bases and selling them from a handcart since the 1960s. A walk next door: Itaewon Bugundang History Park 이태원부군당 역사공원

Best for Sunset · A ten-minute strollWalk from UMMU 2 min · 130 m

Address
37 Noksapyeong-daero 40da-gil (녹사평대로40다길 37)
The story
The street UMMU stands on carries an honorary name, Yu Gwan-sun-gil, after the independence activist Yu Gwan-sun, who died on September 28, 1920 and was buried in the Itaewon public cemetery. When the cemetery was moved in 1936 to make way for a Japanese military base, her remains were lost; in 2015 Yongsan-gu set up a memorial stone to her in this park, which looks out toward where the cemetery once stood. The park takes its name from the bugundang, the village shrine beside it, ringed by zelkova trees more than a hundred years old.
Do this
Go up just before sunset. The viewing deck looks out over Yongsan toward the towers of Yeouido, and after dark N Seoul Tower lights up on Namsan.
Say this in Korean
사진 좀 찍어 주세요Sajin jom jjigeo juseyo · Could you take a photo for us?
Good to know
Free and open to everyone. The shrine itself stays locked except on ritual days; a memorial ceremony for Yu Gwan-sun is held here every September.

A walk: Haebangchon Sinheung Market → Namsan Dulle-gil 해방촌 신흥시장 ~ 남산 둘레길

Best for A half-day walkWalk from UMMU 31 min · 1.6 km uphill to Sinheung Market (or a short taxi ride)

Address
Sinheung Market — 95-9 Sinheung-ro (신흥로 95-9)
The story
Haebangchon, on the slope of Namsan, takes its name from the settlers who built makeshift homes there after Korea's liberation in 1945. Sinheung Market, in the middle of the neighborhood, has operated since 1953 and today mixes old brick buildings and narrow alleys with newer local shops. From the market it's a short walk to the Huam-dong 108-step hillside elevator, and from there to Namsan Dulle-gil, a flat, car-free loop trail around the mountain that's open year-round.
Do this
Start at Sinheung Market, stop by Storage Book & Film (card 11), then take the Huam-dong 108-step hillside elevator up toward the Namsan Dulle-gil trail.
Say this in Korean
남산 둘레길 어느 쪽이에요?Namsan dulle-gil eoneu jjogieyo? · Which way is the Namsan trail?
Good to know
Namsan Dulle-gil is a flat, car-free loop. The walk up from the house is steep, so going by taxi and walking back downhill is the easy way.
