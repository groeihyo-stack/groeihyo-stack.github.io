Your stay

Check-in

From 3 PM. We send your door code by Airbnb message the day before. On the keypad, enter the code and finish with the * key.

Wi-Fi

Network KT_GIGA_45FA. The password is on the sheet by the entrance.

Lights

The round wall switch: press once on/off, press twice to change the color, slide your finger around the ring for brightness.

Floor heating

Turn the right knob on the boiler panel: + warmer, − cooler. Going out? Turn it to 외출 (Away). Please leave the left dial and buttons as they are.

Air conditioning

Remote-control photo coming.

Photo: AC remote control

Bathroom

One bathroom for the whole group: 2 towels per guest and several hair dryers are ready.

Toilet paper only in the toilet — no wet wipes, food or sanitary products. If it clogs, don't keep flushing; message us.

Trash

Three bins, three bags.

General waste → white or green bag with Korean letters.
Recycling → clear bag (rinsed cans, plastic, bottles, paper).
Food waste → yellow bag.

Full bag: tie it and take it to the ground floor outside the entrance after 5 PM. On your check-out morning, tie all bags and leave them just outside the apartment door.

Keep it quiet

Quiet hours 10 PM – 8 AM

Our neighbors live right next to us and below us. Close the windows when you talk at night, keep voices low on the stairs and at the door, no music or parties.

The rooftop is off limits.

House rules

  • No smoking anywhere
  • Shoes off at the entrance
  • Registered guests only, no visitors
  • Max 10 guests

Check-out

By 11 AM. Late check-out until 1 PM: 10,000 KRW per hour — ask us in the morning and we'll confirm.

Contact

Airbnb messages first. For anything urgent, call or text Hyobin: 010-8795-5232.
