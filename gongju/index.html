Chungnam · Baekje Historic Areas

Gongsanseong Fortress, Gongju

A UNESCO-listed royal fortress on a river ridge, a half-day from Seoul, with the town's most-reviewed lunch spots and its chestnut pastry within a five-minute walk of the gate.

UNESCO 2015Historic Site No. 12Walls 9:00–18:00 (Mar–Oct)

Why goThe capital of an old Korean kingdom, still walkable

From 475 to 538, Gongju was the royal capital of the Baekje kingdom, known as Ungjin Baekje. Gongsanseong was its royal fortress, an earthen wall built along the ridges and valleys of a hill at about 110 m elevation beside the Geum River. In the Joseon era it was rebuilt in stone.

You walk the top of the wall itself. From the ramparts the whole town of Gongju lies in view below you.

The main entrance is Geumseoru, the west gate. A guard-changing ceremony is held there; the schedule is posted on the Gongju city site.

In 2015 UNESCO listed the Baekje Historic Areas: eight sites across three towns (two in Gongju, four in Buyeo, two in Iksan). Gongju's two are this fortress and the Royal Tombs in Songsan-ri.

A half-day planWalls, lunch, chestnuts, market

    Enter at Geumseoru and walk the walls

    About 10:00 · 1.5–2 hours

    Follow the wall around the fortress. Expect some climbing up and down.

  2. 02

    Lunch

    About 12:00

    Pick one of three: Maehyang Makguksu, Sijang Jeongyukjeom Sikdang or Gomanaru 1999. All three are within about five minutes of the gate.

  3. 03

    Chestnut pie or coffee

    About 13:30

    Bakery Bammaeul for the chestnut pie, or Gongdabang for coffee with the wall in view.

    Stroll Sanseong Market

Where to eat and sitSix places within a short walk

Each is within about a 15-minute walk of Geumseoru, has 100+ Google reviews, and does something different from the others. Distances are approximate. What we describe below is what Korean reviewers say again and again.

Buckwheat noodles

Maehyang Makguksu

매향 막국수

About 300 m · 5 min walk from the gate

Why go. Reviewers say the noodles are 100% buckwheat, ground in-house. Reviewers keep coming back to one combo: bibim makguksu (spicy mixed noodles) with pyeonyuk-muchim, thin slices of boiled beef tossed in perilla oil. Perilla-oil makguksu and pyeongyang naengmyeon are also on the menu.

Good to knowMon, Wed–Fri 11:00–15:00; Sat–Sun 10:30–15:00; closed Tuesday. Order on a tablet and pay up front. Weekends and holidays see a line before opening, though reviewers say it moves quickly. A few reviewers say weekday lines are short.
Open in Google Maps · 1,055 reviews, 4.3

Beef tartare bibimbap

Sijang Jeongyukjeom Sikdang

시장정육점식당

About 330 m · 5 min walk from the gate

Why go. The menu is effectively one dish: yukhoe bibimbap made with raw Korean beef and shredded raw Gongju chestnut. Reviewers repeatedly mention the crunch of the chestnut. A bowl of seonji-guk (beef blood soup) comes alongside. Reviewers say food arrives quickly after you order, and Korean reviewers note it was featured on a TV food show.

Good to knowWed–Sun 11:00–15:00 and 17:00–20:00; closed Monday and Tuesday. The dish includes cucumber, so say so in advance if you don't want it. You take your shoes off to go in.
Open in Google Maps · 743 reviews, 4.3

Korean set meal

Gomanaru 1999

고마나루1999

About 200 m · 3 min walk from the gate

Why go. One set menu, the Gomanaru jeongsik: pot rice, grilled hwangtae (dried pollack), boiled pork, spicy stir-fried pork and side dishes. Reviewers say the leafy greens and side dishes at the self-serve bar are refillable. It sits directly across from the fortress, and reviewers mention seeing the fortress through the windows. Many describe a clean interior and a place for family meals.

Good to knowOpen daily 11:00–21:00. Reviewers report waits at weekend lunch and say weekend reservations are not possible.
Open in Google Maps · 397 reviews, 4.1

Chestnut pastry

Bakery Bammaeul (Chestnut Town)

베이커리 밤마을

About 180 m · 3 min walk from the gate

Why go. Gongju is chestnut country: the city site lists a chestnut festival and a chestnut industry expo, and reviewers call chestnuts a local specialty. The bakery's signature is the bam pie, a crisp pastry with a whole Gongju chestnut inside, and reviewers also keep mentioning the chestnut eclair. Reviewers say you can see the fortress from the second-floor window seats.

Good to knowOpen daily 9:00–21:00. Weekend lines get long, but reviewers say they clear fast because many customers take away. Some reviewers say they buy it as gifts.
Open in Google Maps · 1,092 reviews, 4.2

Café

Gongdabang

공다방

About 160 m · 2 min walk from the gate

Why go. It faces the fortress entrance, and reviewers say its floor-to-ceiling windows frame Geumseoru and the wall. Many also mention the modern interior. A couple of reviewers say the wall looks good from here once the lights come on after dark.

Good to knowTue–Fri and Sun 11:00–21:00; Sat 11:00–22:00; closed Monday. Reviewers say the window seats are few and fill quickly.
Open in Google Maps · 213 reviews, 4.4

Traditional market

Gongju Sanseong Traditional Market

공주 산성시장

About 760 m · 12 min walk from the gate

Why go. A traditional market a walk south of the fortress. Reviewers mention chestnut snacks, Korean street snacks and small restaurants. Reviewers also mention a five-day market and a summer evening night market.

Good to knowOpen daily 6:00–20:00 on Google Maps; a couple of reviewers say the restaurants tend to close in the early evening. The five-day market is said to fall on days ending in 1 and 6. The summer night market is said to run on Friday and Saturday evenings; it changes from year to year, so check locally.
Open in Google Maps · 2,601 reviews, 4.0

Add-onRoyal Tombs in Songsan-ri

Gongju's other UNESCO site

Royal Tombs in Songsan-ri

무령왕릉과 왕릉원

The Tomb of King Muryeong is the only royal tomb from Korea's Three Kingdoms period whose occupant is positively identified. It was excavated in 1971.

Good to knowSame hours as the fortress. Free parking. By city bus 101 or 125 from Gongju Bus Terminal, get off at Gongju Culture Center. From the fortress it is a short taxi ride or about a 30-minute walk.
